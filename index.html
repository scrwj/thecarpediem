Who this serves

The KNUST College of Health Sciences community, across the whole fraternity.

HESA-KNUST — the umbrella student association for the health sciences fraternity, an estimated ~8,000 students.
Every profession has a place. Medicine, MLS, Nursing, Pharmacy, Dentistry, Physiotherapy, Midwifery, Imaging, Public Health, Nutrition, Vet Medicine and more.
Mobile first, low data, no friction. PDFs that download cleanly, pages that open on any phone browser, nothing that forces an install.
