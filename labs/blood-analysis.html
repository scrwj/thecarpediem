Patient Intake

Select a patient to admit. Ground-truth physiology is generated but hidden (visible via HEXA_BUS.truth() or ?debug=1).

Step 1 — Venipuncture (Blood Draw)

Pick the correct evacuated tube for the ordered panel, then draw 10 mL.

Step 2 — Centrifugation

Spin the sample. Whole blood separates into plasma / buffy coat / packed RBCs. Proper separation needs roughly ≥ 1,500 × g for ≥ 5 minutes.

Step 3 — Assays

Read each result off the instrument / manual method and record it. Values you record feed the diagnosis engine.

🧪 Hematocrit (micro-capillary, %PCV)

Read packed-cell column height ÷ total column × 100

🩸 Hemoglobin (g/dL)

Cyanmethemoglobin method

🧫 WBC count (×10⁹/L)

Improved Neubauer chamber

🌡️ ESR (mm/hr — Westergren)

Requires citrate (blue) tube

🧈 Serum cholesterol (mg/dL)

Requires plain (red) tube — serum
