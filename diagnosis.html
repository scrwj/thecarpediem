The idea

Students investigate patient cases, request or review tests, interpret findings, reason through differential diagnoses and see how different healthcare professionals contribute to care.

Cross-disciplinary by design. Medicine, Medical Laboratory Science, Nursing, Pharmacy, Dentistry, Physiotherapy, Midwifery, Medical Imaging, Public Health, Nutrition & Dietetics, Occupational Therapy, Veterinary Medicine and other relevant fields.
“Every profession has a place. Not every case requires every profession.” Different cases pull in different expertise — that's the point.
Extraction-ready labs. The virtual lab prototypes already emit a structured event trace (draw → spin → assay → reasoning) that the engine will consume.
✅ Playable cases are live (educational simulations). More profession-specific experiences, deeper procedure/equipment guides, progress tracking, leaderboards and voice-enabled features are still being built — each ships only when it’s real and tested.
