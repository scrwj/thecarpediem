1 · Quick start (three clicks)

  1. Double-click CarpeDiem_Genetics_MegaQuiz.html. Nothing to install, no internet needed.
  2. On the welcome page, tap Enter the modules — pick a module (or Grand Mix), then a section.
  3. Choose Explain As I Go or Exam Mode, pick a timer or go untimed — and start.

2 · Welcome page, then the banks

  • Welcome page — the quiz opens quietly: badge, a short hello ("this is the Carpe Diem Genetics engine"), the span of the bank (810 questions · Modules 1–9 + Practice Paper · 50 sections · Easy/Medium/Hard/Devil), and one gold Enter the modules button. No wall of questions at first glance.
  • Where you stopped — if you left a run early, a highlighted card appears on the welcome page with Continue and a ✕ to delete that progress. The same card sits at the top of each module you revisit.
  • The banks (after Enter) — Grand Mix — all 810 plus every module, grouped by course block; each row shows slide count, question count, your best score there, and an Open button.
  • Leaderboard / Settings stay as two quiet buttons under the welcome card.

3 · Choosing what to play

Each module offers its sections (matching your lecture slides) plus Whole module — one straight pass through every question in lecture order. Section names are exactly the headings from the question banks, e.g. Meiosis I or The Y chromosome, SRY and TDF. Seven themes include the Diagnosis Engine's Sunset and Forest palettes.

4 · Modes and the timer

Explain As I Go

After every answer you get instant feedback and (usually) the explanation for that option, plus the EUPHEME commentator's line. You can't change an answer once picked — that's the point. Every answer is autosaved, so closing the tab keeps your place.

Exam Mode

No feedback until the end — answer all, then see the grade, every miss and the full review. Also autosaves.

Timer

Optional. The app recommends a pace: Easy 40s Medium 1 min Hard 90s Devil 2 min per question. Change the minutes box, or tap Start without timer. If time runs out mid-run, your run is graded on what's answered and you land on results.

5 · Playing a run

  • Next only unlocks once the current question is answered (in the forward direction).
  • Back lets you re-read earlier questions (answers stay locked).
  • Save & exit parks the run; it reappears as a Continue card on the home and module screens.
  • The gold progress bar shows how far you are; the clock, if timed, turns red under one minute.
  • Question-type hints appear when relevant — e.g. multiple completion (each option is a combination of numbered statements).
Practice paper questions run key-only: the bank ships an official answer key without explanations, so no "why" is shown — your review simply marks answer key next to the right letter. Everything is still graded.

6 · Results screen

  • Big A–F grade, score, percent and a verdict line from your EUPHEME pack.
  • Review your N missed — every wrong one with the right answer highlighted.
  • Full review — every question — the whole section with explanations, in order.
  • Device leaderboard for that module — type a nickname and Rename my score to stamp it on your latest result.
  • Play this section again, or head home.
The device board keeps your top scores per module on this browser/device. A global board activates automatically once a Google Apps Script URL is configured (§9).

8 · The grand tour math

810 questions in total, spread easy 81 · medium 204 · hard 324 · devil 201. Question types: single-best 698, multiple completion 7, classification 5, and 100 key-only practice-paper items. Grades: A ≥ 70%, B ≥ 60, C ≥ 50, D ≥ 40, else F.

9 · Global leaderboard (optional)

Out of the box scores live only on your device. To share a global board: open the Google Apps Script flow described in leaderboard_apps_script.gs in this folder, deploy it, paste the resulting /exec URL into the template's BANK.config.leaderboard_url and rebuild with python build_genetics_megaquiz.py build. Until then, nothing breaks — the app just stays local.

10 · Good to know

  • Offline forever. Badge and monogram are embedded into the file; fonts are your system's; sounds are synthesised on the fly.
  • Autosave. Every answer is written to that browser's localStorage. Changing browsers or clearing site data resets progress and boards.
  • One device, many yous? The nickname only stamps the newest score per module — old entries keep their name.
  • Wrong end-houseKeeping? If the page looks stale after a rebuild, hold Ctrl+F5 to reload from disk.
