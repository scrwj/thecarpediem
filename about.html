About THE CARPE DIEM

SEIZE THE SEMESTER

Seize the day — even the gnarly ones. Especially the gnarly ones.

What it is

An independent, student-led initiative. It began as a WhatsApp outreach and support space for incoming Health Sciences students at KNUST — programme information, honest answers, and a way to connect before and during the transition to campus. It has grown into a broader learning, resource, community and engagement platform.
Core idea: make useful information, learning resources and student opportunities easier to access — practical, accessible, engaging and community-oriented rather than merely promotional.
The motto — THE BEAUTY IN THE GNARLY. "Carpe diem" is commonly translated "seize the day." This initiative's reading of it: there is beauty in the gnarly — the hard courses, the crowded schedules, the first year nobody warns you about. Pluck it anyway.
Honest by design. Official HESA/KNUST content is labelled official. Peer-created resources are labelled peer-created. This initiative is independent — it is not an official HESA department, committee or endorsed platform.

What we do NOT do

Boundaries are part of the brand.

We don't publish official HESA announcements — official notices live on knust.edu.gh and verified HESA channels, and we link to them.
We don't present peer-made notes or question banks as official examination materials. They're learning aids. Check them against your lecturers and official sources.
We don't publish private contact details, student records or identifiable photos without consent. Report anything inaccurate via the corrections route.
We don't favour one programme. Every profession in the fraternity has a place; no case or feature centres a single discipline.
📚 About HESA-KNUST (official context): the Health Sciences Students' Association is the umbrella body representing students within the health sciences fraternity at KNUST's College of Health Sciences (est. 2005) — an estimated ~8,000 students across Medicine, Dentistry, Pharmacy, Nursing & Midwifery, Allied Health, Public Health, Herbal and Veterinary Medicine. Read more at the College's student associations page.
