Gemini Student Access — request an appointment

INDEPENDENT STUDENT INITIATIVE

THE CARPE DIEM helps students navigate Google's Ghana student AI offer and arranges in-person assistance with the payment-method step. Anyone can request a slot — we do not turn anyone away for age. Google makes the final decision on the offer itself; we are not Google, KNUST or HESA.

What this is

The current Ghana offer is Google AI Plus for 12 months at no subscription charge during the promotional period, subject to Google's eligibility and terms. It automatically renews at GHS 70/month after the offer unless cancelled earlier. A qualifying payment method is required at sign-up.

Who can request help

Any student — we don't restrict by age. You just need a personal Google account and a way to be on the call. Google decides final eligibility for the offer itself (it normally expects 18+, current enrolment, SheerID verification and a qualifying payment method), but under-18 students are welcome to request a slot and try. We'll help however Google allows.

What the GHS 5 is

The GHS 5 is charged by Google as part of the sign-up — it is not a Carpe Diem fee and we do not collect it. Google advertises 12 months at no subscription charge during the promotional period; confirm the exact amount on Google's own checkout screen before you pay.

What we never ask for

Never your Google password, verification code/OTP, Ghana Card number or photo, card number, CVV or bank details. You operate your own phone and account; the facilitator only assists with the payment step.

How it works

Submitting this form is a request — not a confirmed appointment.

1

Request

Fill the form with your details and a preferred day and 30-minute window.

2

Review

The facilitator checks hall density, travel and availability, then approves, proposes another time or declines.

3

Confirm

You get a confirmation with the agreed window and meeting point. Nothing is confirmed before that.

4

Meet & assist

Bring your phone, account access, proof of enrolment as Google asks, and your Ghana Card for our check. Google's GHS 5 charge is handled on Google's side during sign-up.
